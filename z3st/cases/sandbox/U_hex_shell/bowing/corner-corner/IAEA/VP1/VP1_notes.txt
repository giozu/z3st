IAEA VP1 - IWGFR/75 App. 2, EXAMPLE 1: single wrapper, linear thermal gradient
================================================================================

Two cases on the same mesh, BCs and temperature:
  VP1/                       constant 15-15Ti (IAEA VP1 values)
  VP1/1515Ti_Tdependent/     T-dependent 15-15Ti (materials/1515Ti_Tdep.yaml, Sobolev)
They are compared with each other and with the beam solution, not with the
IAEA/IWGFR benchmarks.

Case
----
  geometry   D = 0.153464101 m (outer circumscribed), t = 3 mm, H = 4 m, orientation vertical
             mid-wall corner-to-corner depth 0.150 m; mesh nf 8, nt 1, nz 400 (hex, 38496 nodes)
  BC         thermal: Dirichlet T(y, z) on face1..6 and face1_in..6_in (string expression)
             mechanical: bottom fully clamped (u = 0)
  T          T(y, z) = 673.15 + clip((z - 1.5) / 1.0, 0, 1) * (125 - 25 y / 0.075)   (K)
             400 C below the core, linear ramp across it (1.5-2.5 m), constant above;
             above the core 500 C at the cold corner (+y), 550 C at the hot corner (-y)
             nt = 1: every node lies on a Dirichlet face, so T is the expression itself
  solver     3d, thermal and mechanical on, linear (direct_mumps), 1 static step at t = 0, xdmf output

  material   VP1/15_15Ti_const.yaml        E 1.7e+11 Pa, nu 0.3, alpha 1.8e-5 1/K, T_ref 400 C
             1515Ti_Tdependent/1515Ti_Tdep_local.yaml
                                           E(T) = E_Sobolev, 167.2 GPa at 400 C, 154.3 GPa at 550 C
                                           eps_th = (eta(T) - eta(T_ref)) / (1 + eta(T_ref)), eta Sobolev
                                           (secant alpha 400-550 C = 1.93e-5 1/K), T_ref 400 C
                                           k, cp Sobolev; nu 0.3; no alpha in the card

Dependencies and functions called
---------------------------------
  z3st, models/thermal_model.py
    Dirichlet 'temperature' string   eval in x, y, z, np -> Function on V_t, constant in time
  z3st, core/spine.py
    E, k, cp strings                 resolved as module.func, built as UFL on T_iter
  z3st, models/mechanical_model.py
    alpha (T - T_ref) I              constant card
    eigenstrain hook                 1515Ti card: steel_1515Ti_Tdep.eigenstrain(T, material)
                                     -> thermal_strain(T, T_ref, expansion_law)
  z3st, utils/writer.py
    _temperature_prescribed(problem)   True when the thermal Dirichlet BCs constrain every
                                     T dof (owned dofs counted, summed over the MPI ranks);
                                     HeatFlux is then not written and the log reports:
      [INFO] HeatFlux not written: every temperature dof is Dirichlet-constrained, so T is
      the imposed field and -k grad T is not a solved heat flux.
  materials/steel_1515Ti_Tdep.py
    E_Sobolev(T), k_Sobolev(T), cp_Sobolev(T), eta_Sobolev(T), thermal_strain(T, T_ref, law)

  VP1/non-regression.py  (z3st.utils.non_regression, utils_extract_xdmf)
    beam_uy(z)          closed form, kappa0 = alpha dT/dy, dT/dy = 50 K / 0.150 m
    von_mises(S)
    section mean of u_y at every node level; metric() at the stations, tracked() for von Mises; finish()
  1515Ti_Tdependent/non-regression.py  (as above + steel_1515Ti_Tdep)
    temperature(y, z)   T of boundary_conditions.yaml
    midwall_hexagon()   points and ds on the mid-wall line, corners at +-R on y
    beam_uy(z)          kappa(z) from the plane-section fit eps ~ a + b y weighted by E(T) ds,
                        kappa = -b, u_y = int kappa (z - s) ds (trapezoid, 4001 points)

Closed form
-----------
  u_y(z) = int_0^z kappa(s) (z - s) ds,  kappa = alpha dT/dy r(z),  r = clip(z - 1.5, 0, 1)
  constant:  u_y = kappa0 (z - 1.5)^3 / 6 (core), kappa0 [1/6 + (z - 2.5)/2 + (z - 2.5)^2/2] (above)
             core top 1.000, ACLP 3.250, duct top 12.250 mm (as verification/fuel_assembly/IAEA/vp1)
  Tdep:      same integral, kappa from the E(T)-weighted fit of eps_th(T) on the section;
             reduces to the constant one for constant alpha and E
  T linear in y in every section: nearly stress-free bow (the y·z term over the core and the
  kinks at z = 1.5 and 2.5 m leave ~3 MPa), so E(T) has almost no effect on u.

Limits
------
  - T is fixed on every node: the thermal equation is never solved, k and cp have no effect,
    and -k grad T would not be a balanced flux (about 6 kW/m2 through the slanted faces,
    about -1.5 kW/m3 from dk/dT |grad T|^2). HeatFlux is therefore not written
    (utils/writer.py, _temperature_prescribed(), see below).
  - eta Sobolev is not the IAEA alpha: the Tdep bow is 8 % larger by definition.
  - The T expression is written in boundary_conditions.yaml of both cases and in
    1515Ti_Tdependent/non-regression.py.
