Automatic differentiation in Z3ST
=================================

Z3ST uses automatic differentiation in the places listed below. It does not
compute adjoint sensitivities of the finite-element solution with respect to
input parameters.

Jacobians of the weak forms
---------------------------

Material properties given as Python functions (``k: materials.fuel_thermal.k``,
``E: materials.zircaloy_E.E``, ``Gc: materials.oxide.Gc``) return UFL
expressions that enter the weak forms. Non-linear residuals are solved by
Newton's method in PETSc SNES through ``dolfinx.fem.petsc.NonlinearProblem``,
which forms the Jacobian by UFL symbolic differentiation of the residual. This
applies to the Neo-Hookean model (stress from ``ufl.diff`` of the strain energy
in :mod:`z3st.models.mechanical_model`), J2 plasticity, creep and the stress
functions supplied by a material card with ``constitutive: custom``.

The case ``verification/plasticity/crystal_single_grain`` uses this route: its
single-crystal law (``single_crystal_law.py`` in the case directory) is a UFL
expression for the stress, and its Newton Jacobian is generated by UFL.

Consistent tangent of creep
---------------------------

:mod:`z3st.models.creep_model` integrates Norton and irradiation creep by
backward Euler with a radial return. A cell-wise numpy Newton computes the
plastic multiplier :math:`\Delta\gamma_0`, and the UFL expression carries one
symbolic Newton step from it,
:math:`\Delta\gamma(u) = \Delta\gamma_0 - g(\Delta\gamma_0, u)/g'(\Delta\gamma_0, u)`.
At convergence the correction vanishes, and ``ufl.derivative`` of this
expression gives the consistent tangent :math:`-(\partial g/\partial u)/g'`.
The cases ``verification/fuel/creep``, ``creep_relaxation``,
``creep_irradiation`` and ``creep_shrink_fit_2D`` check the result.

Neural-network conductivity
---------------------------

A material card may define ``k`` with ``type: neural_network`` and a PyTorch
weights file (:mod:`z3st.models.nn_conductivity`). The model returns :math:`k`
and :math:`\mathrm{d}k/\mathrm{d}T`, the derivative computed by PyTorch
automatic differentiation. With ``thermal.solver: newton`` the conductivity
enters the form as an external operator of ``dolfinx-external-operator``
(``make_external_operator``): it is evaluated at the quadrature points and
:math:`\mathrm{d}k/\mathrm{d}T` enters the Newton tangent. The Gaussian-process
and Magni conductivity models (:mod:`z3st.models.gpr_conductivity`,
:mod:`z3st.models.magni_conductivity`) provide the same two methods, with
:math:`\mathrm{d}k/\mathrm{d}T` in closed form. Case:
``verification/thermal/nn_conductivity_slab_2D``.

Forward-mode differentiation for parameter identification
---------------------------------------------------------

``verification/fuel/creep_law_discovery/discover.py`` identifies a creep law
from stress-relaxation data generated by Z3ST. It integrates the relaxation
ODE at a material point and propagates the derivatives of the stress with
respect to the logarithms of the law coefficients through every implicit step
with dual numbers (forward mode), implemented in the script. The derivatives
feed a Gauss-Newton fit. The finite-element solver is used only to generate
the data. See :doc:`in_development`.

Posterior propagation
---------------------

``studies/gpr_uq_margin`` does not differentiate. It reruns a coupled
thermal solve with the conductivity shifted by :math:`\xi = -2 \dots 2`
posterior standard deviations of the Gaussian-process correction and reports
the change of the centre temperature and of the margin to the MOX solidus.
